01 / START HERE

Understand the test before you study for it.

We’ll give you the map first: what the LSAT measures, how the current test works, and where to begin.

01

What is the LSAT?

A skills-based admissions test focused on reasoning and reading—not memorized legal knowledge.

02

Current structure

Logical Reasoning and Reading Comprehension sections, plus an unscored experimental section.

03

How scoring works

Your scaled score reflects correct answers. There is no penalty for guessing.

04

How long to study

Most students benefit from 8–16 weeks, adjusted for time, baseline, and target score.

03 / TEST STRATEGY

A better process beats frantic effort.

Strategy is not a bag of tricks. It is a repeatable way to spend your time, learn from mistakes, and stay calm.

Timing & skippingKnow when to move on so one hard question doesn’t take your test with it.
GuessingMake every unanswered question a considered guess. There is no guessing penalty.
Blind reviewSeparate what you know from what time pressure made difficult.
Error logsTurn missed questions into patterns you can actually fix.
Practice-test reviewReview the why—not just the right answer—and track recurring weaknesses.
Test-day strategyPrepare your environment, schedule, breaks, and mindset before test day.
